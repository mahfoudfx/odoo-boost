# Repository instructions

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for development conventions.

When adding a learning source, extracting lessons from books or example code, or
resuming a source review, follow [the internal learning workflow](docs/source-learning-workflow.md).
Update its source register, coverage records and extraction ledger before finishing
each review batch. End the response with what was extracted, its project benefit,
what changed (if anything), and remaining verification or coverage gaps.
